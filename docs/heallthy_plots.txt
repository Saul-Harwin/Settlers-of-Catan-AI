What a Healthy Training Curve Would Look Like

Ideal trends during reinforcement learning training (e.g., PPO):

Policy Loss
    Small oscillations around a relatively stable magnitude. Large spikes or persistent 
    drift may indicate unstable policy updates or excessive clipping.

Value Loss
    Gradually decreases over time as the critic learns to approximate the value function 
    more accurately. Some noise is expected, but the long-term trend should decline.

Entropy
    Slowly decreases during training. Early training should have higher entropy 
    (encouraging exploration), while later training should show lower entropy as the 
    policy becomes more confident.

KL Divergence
    Remains relatively small and stable. Large spikes indicate that policy updates are 
    too aggressive and may violate the PPO trust region assumption.

Mean Episode Reward
    Shows an overall upward trend with decreasing variance as the agent improves and 
    learns a more consistent policy.

Interpretation
    If training curves show partial improvement but unstable value loss, it often suggests 
    the critic is not learning reliably. This can lead to noisy advantage estimates and 
    unstable policy updates.
