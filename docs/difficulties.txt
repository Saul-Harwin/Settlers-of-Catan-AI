Summary of the Three Most Critical Requirements

For PPO to learn Catan effectively:
    - Correct credit assignment
        reward given to acting player

    - Masked action sampling
        invalid logits → -inf
        
    - Player-relative state representation
        state encoded from current player's perspective - For reinforcement learning algorithms such as 
        Proximal Policy Optimization, this symmetry reduction significantly improves sample efficiency 
        when training agents for Settlers of Catan.
